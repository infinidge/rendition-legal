Privacy Policy for Rendition

Last updated: 27.08.2026

Rendition ("the app," "we," "us") is a curated library of AI image-generation prompts. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

The core thing to know

Rendition never collects, stores, uploads, or has access to any photo of you. The app does not include a camera, a photo picker, or any image-generation feature. Prompts are meant to be copied and used in a separate AI tool of your choosing (such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney, or others) — that process happens entirely outside Rendition, on your device and within that other app or service, and we have no visibility into it.

Information We Collect

Rendition does not require an account, login, or any personal information to use.

  • Bookmarked prompts: if you save a prompt, that choice is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to us or to any server, and is lost if you uninstall the app.
  • Notification preferences: if you enable notifications, your device's notification token may be used to deliver a daily "Prompt of the Day" reminder. This is used solely for that purpose.
  • Advertising data: Rendition displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy practices. See Google's Advertising Policy and Google's Privacy Policy for details on what Google collects and how it's used.

How the App's Content Works

The prompts, titles, and example images shown in Rendition are stored in a content database (Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage) that we maintain. When you browse the app, your device reads this content directly — no personal or identifying information is sent as part of that process, and the database only ever grants read access, never write access, to the app.

Children's Privacy

Rendition is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the relevant age of consent in your region). Since the app collects no personal information from any user, this applies equally regardless of age.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since bookmarks and preferences are stored only on your device, uninstalling the app removes them completely. We do not retain any user data on our own servers, because none is collected.

Third-Party Services

Rendition uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (app content delivery)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
